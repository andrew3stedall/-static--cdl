# Collect public evidence

Use this skill to gather information that could alter expected minutes, role, set pieces, team strength, availability or draft priority.

## Source focus

Prioritise:

1. official club accounts and direct quotations;
2. reliable club correspondents;
3. fixture specialists such as Ben Crellin;
4. tactical analysts such as James from Planet FPL;
5. concise FPL information accounts such as Sam Martin;
6. informed fan accounts for local observations;
7. broader FPL creators only as secondary consensus checks.

Information density matters more than creator popularity. Do not reproduce long creator commentary when a concise underlying fact is available.

## Evidence capture

For every material item record:

- source/account;
- exact post or article URL;
- publication timestamp and timezone when available;
- retrieval timestamp in Australia/Melbourne;
- affected player(s), team(s) and position(s);
- factual claim or observation;
- source class and reliability;
- whether corroborated;
- ranking implication;
- confidence: confirmed, strong report, credible observation, weak signal or speculation.

## Rules

- Link the specific post, not merely the account profile.
- Prefer paraphrase; quote only the minimum wording needed.
- Do not treat repetition across aggregator accounts as independent corroboration.
- Record relevant evidence that was considered but did not change the board.
- Separate what the source said from the agent's inference.
