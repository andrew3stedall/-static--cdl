# Assess preseason evidence

Preseason matters primarily because it reveals role and expected minutes, not because friendly-match points predict league output directly.

## Evidence weighting

Weight most heavily:

1. starts and minutes with the probable first team;
2. position and tactical role;
3. set-piece and penalty duties;
4. manager comments and repeated patterns;
5. competition for places and substitution order;
6. chance involvement and underlying role;
7. goals and assists, adjusted for opposition and match context.

## Procedure

For each relevant player, record:

- match and date;
- opponent and strength/context;
- starting group quality;
- minutes;
- nominal and actual position;
- set pieces;
- notable tactical interactions;
- fitness or managed-minutes context;
- whether the signal repeats prior evidence;
- confidence and draft implication.

## Guardrails

- Do not promote a player substantially for isolated returns against weak opposition.
- Do promote meaningful evidence of a newly secure role, central positioning, penalties or reduced competition.
- Distinguish experimental line-ups from probable league structures.
- Record uncertain interpretations in the watchlist rather than forcing a ranking move.
