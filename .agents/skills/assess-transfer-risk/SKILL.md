# Assess transfer risk

Monitor Fabrizio Romano, official club announcements and reliable club reporters. The objective is not transfer-news completeness; it is ranking impact.

## Confidence ladder

- **Confirmed:** official registration or club announcement.
- **Near-certain:** credible "here we go" or equivalent agreement-complete reporting.
- **Advanced:** negotiations or agreement reported as advanced but unresolved.
- **Interest:** club interest, enquiry or monitoring.
- **Speculation:** weak, recycled or uncorroborated links.

## Procedure

For each development, assess:

1. probability and expected timing;
2. whether the player remains in the Premier League and FPL pool;
3. likely role at the destination;
4. effect on the player's current draft value;
5. effect on incumbents, backups and teammates;
6. whether a departure creates opportunity;
7. whether an arrival threatens minutes, set pieces or tactical role.

## Ranking treatment

- Confirmed and near-certain moves may cause immediate material movement.
- Advanced talks should usually change risk and may change rank.
- Interest belongs on the watchlist unless the downside is unusually asymmetric.
- Speculation should not alter the board without corroboration.

Cite the exact report and preserve the original confidence wording without exaggeration.
