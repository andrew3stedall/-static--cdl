# 2026/27 Initial Positional Scarcity

## League context

Eight managers will select 160 players. The canonical board extends to 220 so replacement level can be judged rather than assuming every ranked player must be drafted.

## Forward

Forward is the clearest scarcity pressure. Proven, high-minute central forwards are difficult to replace, but positional scarcity must not be used to justify uncertain starters or injured players too early.

Initial implications:

- Haaland is the clearest first overall selection.
- Watkins has a durable high-minute profile.
- João Pedro and Igor Thiago have attractive scarcity value but require role confirmation.
- Kroupi's injury materially reduces his draft usefulness despite forward scarcity.
- Lower-table forward competitions should be resolved before treating every probable starter as a top-80 asset.

## Midfielder

Midfield has the greatest numerical depth. The relevant separator is not simply attacking reputation; it is secure minutes plus centrality, penalties, set pieces and repeatable goal involvement.

Initial implications:

- Bruno Fernandes has the strongest combination of role, minutes and set pieces.
- Manchester City midfielders require a substantial rotation discount.
- Deep or defensive midfielders should not outrank elite attackers merely because defensive-contribution projections are favourable without independent validation.
- Midfield availability means managers can usually delay lower-ceiling options.

## Defender

Defenders receive greater value from elite clean-sheet probability, attacking threat and defensive contributions. The first external model places several defenders extremely high, including Gabriel at third overall.

Initial interpretation:

- elite defenders may justify earlier selection than in older FPL scoring environments;
- the exact magnitude is uncertain and may be overstated by the model;
- attacking role, set pieces and secure minutes remain preferable to centre-back name recognition alone;
- Liverpool defensive depth and Arsenal injury flags require monitoring.

## Goalkeeper

Goalkeeper is deep relative to an eight-manager league. The first external ranking incorrectly elevated several possible backups.

Initial implications:

- presumed backup goalkeepers were demoted into the undrafted buffer;
- starting hierarchy matters more than team name;
- goalkeeper runs should not force managers to select uncertain backups;
- a two-goalkeeper roster still leaves replacement options in an eight-team league unless several clubs have unresolved competitions.

## Draft-round guidance

- Rounds 1–4: prioritise scarce elite forwards, elite attacking midfield roles and only genuinely exceptional defenders.
- Rounds 5–10: balance positional needs without chasing model-generated defender runs blindly.
- Rounds 11–16: secure starters and set-piece roles become more valuable than speculative upside.
- Rounds 17–20: use unresolved role upside, promoted-club starters and handcuffs selectively; avoid backup goalkeepers without a clear route to minutes.

## Confidence

The broad scarcity conclusions are medium confidence. Exact replacement levels and cross-position rank gaps remain low-to-medium confidence until the top 80 receive a source-by-source manual review.
