# Source and Citation Policy

## Source hierarchy

1. Official FPL API.
2. Official club, competition, manager and player communications.
3. Reliable club correspondents and specialist reporters.
4. Tactical and fixture specialists.
5. Concise FPL analysts.
6. Informed fan accounts and communities.
7. Aggregators and general commentary.

A lower-ranked source may still be useful, but confidence and corroboration must reflect its limitations.

## Citation requirement

Every material claim must link to the specific supporting item:

- X: direct status URL rather than the account profile;
- article: direct article URL;
- video or podcast: direct episode URL and timestamp when practical;
- API: endpoint plus retrieval timestamp;
- official statement: direct statement URL.

Use Markdown links for external sources and Obsidian links for internal notes.

## Evidence labels

- **Confirmed:** official or directly observable.
- **Strong report:** highly reliable reporting with clear sourcing.
- **Credible observation:** plausible first-hand or specialist observation.
- **Inference:** conclusion derived from cited facts.
- **Weak signal:** potentially relevant but insufficient for a ranking change.
- **Speculation:** retained only when useful to explain why it was rejected.

## Source independence

Ten aggregator posts repeating one original report count as one source. Identify the originating report where possible.

## Quotation

Prefer concise paraphrase. Quote only the wording necessary to preserve meaning, especially for confidence phrases such as "agreement reached" or "advanced talks".

## Unavailable evidence

Record paywalls, deleted posts, inaccessible accounts and search gaps. Do not reconstruct unavailable claims from memory.
