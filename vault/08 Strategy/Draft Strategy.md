# Draft Strategy

## League constraints

- 8 managers.
- 20 rounds.
- 160 total selections.
- Maintain a 220-player board for replacement and waiver context.

## Ranking objective

Maximise expected season-long FPL Draft usefulness. Price, value and ownership are irrelevant unless the league later introduces custom rules that make them relevant.

## Initial segments

| Segment | Picks | Approximate rounds |
|---|---:|---:|
| Franchise | 1–8 | 1 |
| Foundation | 9–32 | 2–4 |
| Core | 33–80 | 5–10 |
| Depth | 81–128 | 11–16 |
| Endgame | 129–160 | 17–20 |
| Undrafted buffer | 161–220 | Waivers/replacements |

## Strategic factors

- Positional scarcity should be assessed against the quality of likely undrafted replacements.
- Secure minutes and durable role generally matter more than short-term hype.
- Transfer, injury and preseason uncertainty should be priced explicitly rather than ignored.
- The board should express an overall priority, while position notes preserve alternative roster-construction paths.
