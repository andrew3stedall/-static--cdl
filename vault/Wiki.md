# FPL Draft Research Wiki

This is the navigational summary of the project. It records durable conclusions and points to detailed evidence; it is not a second draft board.

## League model

- Eight managers.
- Twenty picks per manager.
- 160 players selected.
- The maintained board extends to at least 220 players.
- Ranking optimises expected Draft usefulness rather than price or ownership.

## Current state

The research foundation is established. No evidence-based player ordering has yet been published. See [[01 Current/Current Draft Board]].

## Method

1. Reconcile the official [[09 Data/FPL API|FPL API]] player pool.
2. Collect post-level evidence from official accounts, club reporters, tactical analysts, fixture specialists and informed supporters.
3. Evaluate preseason and transfers separately before combining them into expected minutes and role.
4. Rank at least 220 players with positional scarcity and replacement level considered.
5. Publish an immutable full review and changes record.
6. Update affected player, team, position and source notes.

See [[00 Meta/Repository Contract]] and [[00 Meta/Source Policy]].

## Current research preferences

- James from Planet FPL is a preferred tactical source.
- Fixture prediction specialists are highly valued.
- Sam Martin is useful for concise information.
- Big Man Bakar may be used as an information source, but long-form analysis should be distilled to the underlying fact.
- Fabrizio Romano is monitored for transfer probability and squad knock-on effects.
- Club and fan accounts may supply valuable role information when properly attributed and corroborated.

## Active uncertainties

- The first official player-pool snapshot and top-220 ranking remain to be produced.
- Preseason roles and transfers will create rapid movement before the season.
