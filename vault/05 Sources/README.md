# Source Notes

Create one note per recurring source or account. Record its specialist domain, reliability, known limitations and a timeline of specific cited posts.

The project values high-signal information over popularity. Preferred categories include official clubs, reliable club correspondents, tactical analysts, fixture specialists, concise FPL information accounts and informed supporters.

Every material research claim must still link to the exact post or article under [[00 Meta/Source Policy|Source and Citation Policy]].
